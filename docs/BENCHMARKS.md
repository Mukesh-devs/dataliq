# Benchmarks

Performance budgets come from `BUILD_PLAN.md`. Numbers are recorded per OS
and per version.

**Machines**

- `linux-dev`: Linux container, 4 vCPU, 15 GB RAM, SSD, Ubuntu 24.04.
  (Fewer cores than the 8-core reference laptop in the plan; numbers are
  conservative.)

## Micro benchmarks (criterion)

Run with `cargo bench --profile dev` (dependencies at `opt-level = 2`) or
`cargo bench` (full release profile).

| Benchmark | What it measures | linux-dev (dev profile) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `arrow_ipc_1000_rows_table` | one grid page: `SELECT *` of 1,000 rows × 6 mixed columns from a table, encoded as Arrow IPC | 0.58 ms |
| `arrow_ipc_1000_rows_generated` | same, rows generated by the query itself | 1.02 ms |

_Recorded during v0.1 step 3._
